Chicken Biryani

A fragrant, spiced rice dish layered with marinated chicken and slow-cooked to perfection.

Recipe Info

Prep Time
30 minutes
Cook Time
1 hour
Servings
4 people
Difficulty
Medium

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 cups basmati rice, soaked
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • Spices
    • 2 tsp biryani masala
    • 1 tsp turmeric powder
    • 1 tsp red chili powder
    • 4 green cardamom pods
  • Aromatics
    • 4 cloves garlic, minced
    • 1-inch ginger, grated
    • 2 fresh green chilies, slit
  • Fresh coriander and mint leaves
  • Saffron strands soaked in warm milk

Instructions

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, chili powder, and biryani masala for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Fry the sliced onions in oil until golden brown and crispy. Set half aside for garnish.
  3. In the same oil, add the marinated chicken and cook on medium heat for 15 minutes until partially done.
  4. Parboil the basmati rice with cardamom, cloves, and salt until it is 70% cooked. Drain immediately.
  5. Layer the rice over the chicken in a heavy-bottomed pot. Sprinkle saffron milk, coriander, mint, and reserved fried onions on top.
  6. Cover tightly and cook on the lowest heat for 25–30 minutes. Do not lift the lid during this time.
  7. Let it rest for 10 minutes, then gently fluff and serve hot with raita.
